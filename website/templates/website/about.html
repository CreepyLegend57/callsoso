Who We Are

A circular economy venture, rooted in one city.

Call Soso captures materials that would otherwise be discarded and redirects them into meaningful second lives — socially, environmentally, and economically.

From cardboard and textiles to foam and product returns, we work with businesses, upcyclers, designers, and educators to transform waste into value. Everything is treated as data, story, and infrastructure for an imaginative economy.

Manifesto

The Rooted Manifesto

We honour materials, edible and otherwise. Everything has a second life. We treat surplus as intelligence, culture, and possibility. Circularity isn't compliance, it's creativity. We build systems, not scraps.

Materials are data. Nothing is static. Everything circulates.

Process

How It Works

01

Capture

We partner with organisations to identify surplus materials across their operations — packaging, textiles, offcuts, returns, and more.

02

Match

Materials are matched with internal teams, makers, creatives, and community groups who can reuse them effectively.

03

Transform

Surplus becomes new products, installations, learning tools, or resources — extending material life and reducing waste.

Where We Work

Rooted in Milton Keynes

Call Soso is based in Milton Keynes — a city designed around grids, green spaces, and movement. It's the natural home for a circular economy network.

Milton Keynes, United Kingdom

Our circularity audit, capture, and match operations run across the city region, connecting corporate surplus with makers, designers, and community groups.
